Pro Equipment

The Machines Behind a Real Dry-Out

Household fans move air. Restoration equipment moves moisture — out of walls, floors, and the air itself — and proves the result with readings. This is what rolls off our trucks on every Fishers job:

Truck-Mounted Extraction

High-lift vacuum pulls water out of carpet and padding in a single pass — hundreds of gallons gone before the first fan is even placed.

Air Movers & Axial Fans

Directed high-velocity airflow across wet surfaces speeds evaporation and pushes moisture toward the dehumidifiers instead of deeper into the structure.

LGR Dehumidifiers

Commercial low-grain refrigerant units pull moisture out of the air around the clock, so framing, drywall, and subfloor — not just surfaces — reach dry standard.

Moisture Meters & Thermal Imaging

Pin and pinless meters plus infrared cameras find hidden water behind walls and under floors — and verify dry before anything gets closed up.

Local Knowledge

Why Water Damage Is So Common in Fishers

Fishers grew fast on Hamilton County clay — and most of its homes have basements. That combination writes our call log:

Clay soil that won't drain

Central Indiana's glacial clay-loam holds water against basement walls instead of draining it away. After heavy spring and fall rain, hydrostatic pressure pushes moisture through floor cracks and cove joints — even in homes that "never had water before."

Sump pumps that quit at the worst moment

In most Fishers basements, one pump stands between a dry floor and a flooded one. Pumps fail mid-storm, lose power, or simply can't keep up — which is why flooded-basement cleanup is our most common spring call.

Freeze–thaw winters

Average winter lows around 18°F mean exposed pipes in garages, crawl spaces, and exterior walls can freeze and split. A burst pipe can release hundreds of gallons before anyone notices.

Geist water table & finished basements

Around Geist Reservoir, a high water table keeps foundations damp, while finished basements across Fishers turn small leaks into carpet, drywall, and trim losses fast.
