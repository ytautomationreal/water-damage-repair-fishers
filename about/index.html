About Fishers Water Damage Repair

Fishers Water Damage Repair is a locally operated water damage restoration company serving Fishers and Hamilton County. We do one thing — water — and we do it around the clock: extraction, structural drying, mold remediation, sewage cleanup, and the repairs that follow.

How we're different

  • One call, whole job. No handoffs between a "mitigation company" and a separate rebuild contractor.
  • Documentation-first. Every job produces a photo, readings, and estimate file your insurer can actually use.
  • Verified dry. We close walls when meters say dry — not when the schedule says so.
  • Local knowledge. Hamilton County clay soil, sump-pump dependence, Geist water tables, and Indiana freeze cycles are our weekly reality.

Our promise

Answer fast, extract faster, document everything, and tell you the truth about what can be saved and what can't. That's the whole business model.
