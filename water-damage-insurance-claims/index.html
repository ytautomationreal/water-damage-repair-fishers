Claims Guide

Water Damage Insurance Claims in Fishers

Most water damage claims aren't denied because the damage isn't real — they're underpaid because the file is thin. A few phone photos and a one-line invoice leave the adjuster guessing, and guesses don't favor you.

What usually is (and isn't) covered

  • Usually covered: burst or frozen pipes, sudden appliance failures, water heater ruptures, storm-driven roof leaks, sump pump failure (with the sump/sewage endorsement).
  • Usually not covered: gradual leaks left unaddressed, groundwater seepage, overland flooding (that's flood insurance), mold from long-term neglect.

The documentation package we build for you

  • Time-stamped photos & video of the source and every affected material, before mitigation.
  • Moisture maps showing what was wet, where, and the daily readings until dry.
  • Line-item estimate in the same estimating format insurer software uses.
  • Contents inventory — damaged items photographed, listed, and valued.
  • Adjuster walk-through — we meet your adjuster at your home and walk the scope together.
  • Supplement support — hidden damage found later gets documented and submitted, not absorbed by you.

Your first 24 hours, in order

  • 1. Stop the water (main shut-off) and make the area safe.
  • 2. Call us for extraction — policies require reasonable mitigation.
  • 3. Photograph everything before cleanup if you can do it safely.
  • 4. Open the claim and give your insurer our documentation package.
  • 5. Keep receipts for any emergency expenses (hotel, pumps, fans).

Context: 1 in 60 insured homes files a water or freezing claim each year, and water damage accounts for 22.6% of all homeowner claims (Insurance Information Institute). Adjusters see this daily — a complete file is simply the fastest route to a fair settlement.
