Warning Signs

Signs You Need Water Damage Restoration

Musty odor that won't leave

Hidden moisture in walls or padding is feeding microbial growth.

Warped, cupped, or buckling floors

Hardwood and laminate absorb water and deform — early drying can save them.

Bubbling paint or staining

Water is moving through drywall from a leak you may not have found yet.

Visible mold patches

Growth means moisture has been present for days, not hours.

Higher water bills

A hidden supply-line leak is quietly soaking your structure.

Soggy carpet or damp padding

Padding holds water like a sponge and rarely dries in place.

How We Fix It

Our Water Damage Restoration Process

We start with source control and extraction, then map moisture with meters and thermal imaging so nothing wet hides behind a wall. Air movers and commercial dehumidifiers pull moisture from materials — not just the air — and we take daily readings until everything hits dry standard. Then repairs: drywall, flooring, trim, paint. Every step is photographed and logged for your claim.
