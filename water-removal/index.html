Warning Signs

Signs You Need Water Removal & Extraction

Standing water anywhere indoors

Even a half-inch spreads under walls and into adjacent rooms.

Sump pump failure during a storm

Water rises fast once the pump quits — call before it reaches the water heater and furnace.

Carpet that squishes underfoot

The padding beneath is saturated even if the surface feels merely damp.

Water heater or washer let go

Appliance tanks dump 40–80 gallons in minutes.

Water creeping toward electrical

Panel, outlets, or appliances near water = call now, don't wade in.

Basement drain backing up

Rising drain water may be sewage — treat it as a health hazard.

Pro Equipment

Equipment We Bring for Water Removal & Extraction

Professional drying is a measured process, not a fan in the corner. The machines and instruments that show up on this job:

Truck-Mounted Extraction

High-lift vacuum pulls water out of carpet and padding in a single pass — hundreds of gallons gone before drying even starts.

Air Movers & Axial Fans

Directed high-velocity airflow speeds evaporation and pushes moisture toward the dehumidifiers instead of deeper into the structure.

LGR Dehumidifiers

Commercial low-grain refrigerant units pull moisture from the air around the clock until framing and subfloor reach dry standard.
