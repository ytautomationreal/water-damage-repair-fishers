Warning Signs

Signs You Need Mold Remediation

Visible spots on walls, ceilings, or grout

What you see is usually a fraction of the colony.

Musty, earthy odor

Often the first sign of hidden growth behind walls or under floors.

Allergy symptoms that ease away from home

A classic household pattern worth investigating.

Past leak or flood in the same area

Any water event older than 48 hours is a mold candidate.

Peeling paint or warped baseboards

Moisture is feeding something behind the surface.

Condensation on windows and pipes

Chronic humidity grows surface mold in bathrooms and basements.

How We Fix It

Our Mold Remediation Process

We inspect and moisture-map first, then build containment with negative air and HEPA filtration so remediation doesn't spread spores through your home. Contaminated porous materials are removed safely, structural surfaces are cleaned and treated, and the moisture source — leak, seepage, humidity — is fixed and verified dry. Optional third-party testing can confirm clearance.

Pro Equipment

Equipment We Bring for Mold Remediation

Professional drying is a measured process, not a fan in the corner. The machines and instruments that show up on this job:

HEPA Air Scrubbing & Containment

Sealed containment and HEPA filtration keep spores and contaminants out of the clean parts of your home while work is underway.

Moisture Meters & Thermal Imaging

Pin and pinless meters plus infrared cameras find hidden water behind walls and under floors — and verify dry before anything is closed up.

LGR Dehumidifiers

Commercial low-grain refrigerant units pull moisture from the air around the clock until framing and subfloor reach dry standard.
